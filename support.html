Loopwise support

Questions, bugs, ideas: [rmthrtdeveloper@gmail.com]

Please include your phone model, its Android or iOS version, and your amp.

The app says the amp is not detected

Plug the amp into the phone with a USB-C data cable: some cables only charge. The status line under the title turns green when the link works.

No sound is recorded

Allow the microphone permission: the phone treats the amp’s USB input as a microphone. The app does not use the phone’s own microphone.

My overdubs are late or early

Open the settings, then “Calibrate latency”. If your amp has USB Loopback, use it; otherwise, play along with the beeps.

The sound crackles

Turn off “Low-latency audio” in the settings.

Where are my loops?

In the library, with the button at the top of the main screen. They are stored only on your phone.

Privacy

Loopwise collects no data. See the privacy policy.
